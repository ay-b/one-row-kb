# Dictionary data: notices

The word lists shipped in `app/src/main/assets/dict/` are compiled from open sources. Their credits and licences are
in **[attribution.md](attribution.md)** (shown in the app under Settings > About > Attributions; the copy the app shows
is `app/src/main/assets/attribution.md`: keep the two identical). How the lists are built is in `tools/dict_src/SOURCES.md`.

Status: settled for release.
- Everything is credited; the share-alike obligation (CC BY-SA 4.0 sources) is met by offering the compiled lists under
  CC BY-SA 4.0.
- The Ukrainian Hunspell dictionary (dict_uk, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 data) is used only as a filter; the owner has decided to treat that
  as not incorporating it (see attribution.md). Replace it with another source if that decision changes.
- The GPL / LGPL / MPL Hunspell dictionaries are used only to vet words; their lists are not copied.
